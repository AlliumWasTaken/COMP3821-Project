Introduction

For our project, we aimed to help people understand the concept behind randomised algorithms. To aide our explanation, we chose to compare randomised and deterministic algorithms, specifically Karger's Algorithm and the Stoer-Wagner Algorithm in their application of solving the global minimum cut problem.

Objectives

Through this comparison, we hope that by the end of our website, you will be able to:
  1. Define an s-t cut, and understand the difference between the global minimum cut problem that we are presenting and the Max-Flow Min-Cut problem.
  2. Understand and be able to explain Graph Contraction.
  3. Run Karger's algorithm by hand, explain why it is a Monte Carlo algorithm, and show how repeating it increases the chance of finding the minimum cut.
  4. Run the Stoer-Wagner algorithm by hand, explain its phase structure and why the cut of the phase is the minimum cut.
  5. Compare the two algorithms by their randomised vs. deterministic properties.
