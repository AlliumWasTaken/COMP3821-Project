Project

For this project, we wanted to create an educational website that aimed to explore the idea of a randomised algorithm. To do so, we decided to make direct comparisons between randomised and deterministic algorithms and their role in solving the global minimum cut problem.

We aim to explain and compare Karger's algorithm and the Stoer-Wagner algorithm in a way that is easily digestible and intuitive, using simple explanations and visual simulations.
